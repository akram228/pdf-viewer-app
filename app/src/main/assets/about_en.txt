PDF Reader

A simple app for reading PDF files on your phone.

Main features
• Swipe pages sideways or scroll up and down
• Bookmarks and jump to any page
• Color filters to keep your eyes comfortable

Privacy
Your files are opened on your phone only. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.
